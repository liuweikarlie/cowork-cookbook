Business context

01

Does this sound familiar?

The customer needs an answer. The context is scattered.

Agents open a complex case and spend the first minutes hunting for customer history, entitlement, SLA and recent conversations. Context lives across CRM, ticketing, email and chat. Incomplete context leads to repeated questions and longer handling. First response quality varies by agent.

02

The workflow to aim for

One intake brief with history, entitlement, and next steps.

Cost Savings: handle time reduced on every complex case. Revenue Growth: better first responses improve CSAT and retention. Risk Mitigation: entitlement and SLA status visible before the agent commits.

Why Cowork?

A complex case begins with context spread across the case system, mail, telemetry and past history. Cowork combines: case and customer context, reconstructed history, entitlement and SLA risk, and a recommended next step, into one intake brief that refreshes when ownership changes.

    Open Cowork and start a new task

  2. 2

    Fill in the brackets

  3. 3

    Copy the prompt and send it

    Copy the prompt, paste it into Cowork, replace the highlighted brackets, then send. This is the original wording from the training deck.

    Prompt · English original
    I need a complete intake brief for case #[Case ID] before I start working it.
Pull the case context:
Use the case system, CRM customer profile, entitlement and SLA data, prior cases, knowledge base material, Outlook and Teams context, call transcripts, and product telemetry where available.
Summarize who the customer is, what they are entitled to, what has already happened, and what changed most recently.
Surface related cases, known product signals, and any prior commitments we already made.
Set me up to act:
Tell me the likely blocker, the immediate next step, and any risk to the SLA or customer relationship.
Call out any missing information I should get before I respond.
Deliverable:
Give me a clear intake brief I can work from immediately, and hold any outbound replies or writebacks for my review.
