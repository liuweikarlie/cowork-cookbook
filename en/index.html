New to Cowork? The idea in 30 seconds

⇄

It works across your apps

Each scenario pulls context from tools you already use — Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Dynamics 365, Excel — only within the access you already have.

≡

It runs a whole workflow

Instead of one chat answer, Cowork moves through several stages — gather, analyze, draft, package — and hands back a finished, review-ready deliverable.

✓

You stay in control

The prompts ask Cowork to hold sends, sharing, publishing, and system updates until you review and approve them.

How to use this cookbook

  1. Pick the situation that sounds like your day
  2. See what it could be worth and what you will get
  3. Check you have the apps and access
  4. Copy the prompt, fill in the brackets, run it
  5. Review everything before you approve
